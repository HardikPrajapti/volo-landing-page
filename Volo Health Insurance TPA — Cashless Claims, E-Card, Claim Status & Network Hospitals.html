Our promise

You'll always know where your claim is — and what happens next.

Every step, who handles it, and how long it takes. No guessing, no chasing.

Cashless claim

At a network hospital — you don't pay upfront

Tell us at least 48 hours before a planned admission, or within 6 hours of an emergency admission.

  1. Show your e-card at the hospital's insurance (TPA) desk

    Carry a photo ID and your policy or employee number.

    You
  2. Hospital sends us the pre-authorisation request

    With the doctor's notes and the treatment cost estimate.

    Hospital
  3. We review and decide

    Approved — or, if something is missing, a query to the hospital so it can be fixed quickly.

    Within 2 hours of complete documents
  4. Treatment and discharge

    The hospital sends the final bill; we approve the final amount as per your policy.

    Volo
  5. Settled with the hospital

    You pay only non-covered items, co-pay or deductions — we tell you which and why.

Reimbursement claim

Paid yourself? Claim it back

For non-network hospitals, or pre- and post-hospitalisation expenses.

  1. Tell us about the hospitalisation

    Intimate on the member portal, WhatsApp or helpline. You get a claim number.

    You
  2. Submit your documents

    Online on the member portal, using the checklist below.

    Within 7 days of discharge
  3. We check and process

    If anything is missing we raise a query — reply quickly to keep things moving.

    Within 10 working days of all documents
  4. Payment to your bank

    Straight to the account on your cancelled cheque.

    Within 5 working days of approval

What your claim status means

And what, if anything, you need to do.

  • RegisteredWe have your claim. Nothing to do.
  • Under processOur medical team is reviewing it.
  • Query raisedAction needed: send the document we asked for.
  • ApprovedApproved by Volo and your insurer.
  • SettledPaid to the hospital or your bank.
  • RejectedWe'll explain why — and how to appeal.

FAQs

Questions people ask most

What is the difference between cashless and reimbursement?

Cashless: at a network hospital, the hospital is paid directly for the covered amount. Reimbursement: you pay first and claim it back — for example at a non-network hospital, or for pre- and post-hospitalisation expenses.

East West Assist is now Volo — does anything change for me?

No. It is the same company with the same IRDAI registration (No. 018). Your policy, cover and e-card stay the same, and cards printed with the EWA name are still valid.

How fast is a cashless approval?

We decide within 2 hours of receiving all documents from the hospital. For planned treatment, ask the hospital to send the request at least 48 hours before admission.

How long does a reimbursement take?

We process it within 10 working days of receiving all documents, and payment reaches your bank within 5 working days of approval.

Why was my claim put on query?

Usually one document is missing or unclear. Your claim status will say what is needed — send it through the member portal or WhatsApp and processing continues.

I lost my e-card. What do I do?

Download it again any time from the member portal or the VOLO-Wallet app, or type “e-card” on WhatsApp.

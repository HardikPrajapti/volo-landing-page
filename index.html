Questions people ask us.

How do I get my e-card?

From the member portal, the VOLO-Wallet app, or by saying “Hi” to our WhatsApp chatbot on +91 95606 84484.

Cashless or reimbursement?

Cashless: at a network hospital, the insurer pays the hospital directly. Reimbursement: you pay and claim it back, for example at a non-network hospital.

When must the hospital ask for cashless?

At least 48 hours before a planned admission, or within 6 hours of an emergency admission.

Which documents does a reimbursement claim need?

Claim form, final bill and receipts, discharge summary, reports and prescriptions, submitted within 7 days of discharge.

How long does reimbursement take?

Processed within 10 working days of receiving all documents, and paid within 5 working days of approval.

What does a TPA do?

An IRDAI-licensed Third Party Administrator services health insurance for the insurer: e-cards, cashless approvals and claims.

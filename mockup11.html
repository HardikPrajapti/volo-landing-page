        Questions people ask us.

        How do I get my e-card?
        From the member portal, the VOLO-Wallet app, or our WhatsApp chatbot on +91 95606 84484.
        Cashless or reimbursement: what's the difference?
        Cashless: the hospital is paid directly. Reimbursement: you pay, then claim it back, for example at a non-network hospital.
        When must the hospital ask for cashless?
        Planned treatment: at least 48 hours before admission. Emergency: within 6 hours of admission.
        Which documents does a reimbursement claim need?
        Claim form, final bill and receipts, discharge summary, reports and prescriptions, within 7 days of discharge.
        How long does reimbursement take?
        Processed within 10 working days of all documents; paid within 5 working days of approval.
        What does a TPA do?
        A Third Party Administrator, licensed by IRDAI, services health insurance for the insurer: e-cards, cashless approvals and claims.

Every claim, one clear journey.

Cashless at a network hospital or reimbursement after you pay, your claim moves through the same five stages, and you can see each one.

  1. Intimated

    You, your family or the hospital tell us about the admission: app, WhatsApp, helpline or the hospital desk.

  2. Documents in

    The hospital sends the pre-authorisation, or you upload your bills after discharge.

  3. Under review

    Our medical team checks the claim against your policy and benefits.

  4. Decision

    Approved, or a query that says exactly which document is needed.

  5. Settled

    Paid to the hospital for cashless, or to your bank for reimbursement.

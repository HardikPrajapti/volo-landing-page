Questions people ask us.

What does a TPA do?
A Third Party Administrator is licensed by IRDAI to service health insurance policyholders for the insurance company: e-cards, cashless approvals and claims.
How do I get my e-card?
Download it from the member portal or the VOLO-Wallet app, or ask our WhatsApp chatbot on +91 95606 84484.
Cashless or reimbursement: what's the difference?
Cashless: the hospital is paid directly for admissible amounts. Reimbursement: you pay, then claim it back, for example at a non-network hospital or for pre- and post-hospitalisation costs.
When must the hospital ask for cashless approval?
For planned treatment, at least 48 hours before admission. In an emergency, within 6 hours of admission.
Which documents does a reimbursement claim need?
Submit within 7 days of discharge on the member portal with the claim form, final bill and payment receipts, discharge summary, reports and prescriptions.
How long does reimbursement take?
Processed within 10 working days of receiving all documents, and paid within 5 working days of approval.
Is treatment outside India covered?
No. Treatment is covered only in India.
What is co-pay?
The share of the admissible amount you pay yourself; the insurer pays the rest.
